Privacy Policy

Featherroll · Effective November 9, 2026

Short version: Featherroll does not collect, upload, sell, or share your photos or any personal data. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad.

Your photos

Featherroll asks for access to your photo library so it can find bursts, duplicates, screenshots, blurry shots, large videos, and items saved by other apps. Photos are analyzed on the device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Nothing is deleted unless you confirm. Confirmed items move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can recover them for about 30 days.

Data stored on your device

Featherroll keeps a small file on your device with scan results and your keep or delete choices, so it does not need to rescan every time. It contains no copies of your photos. Deleting the app removes it.

Data we collect

None. Featherroll has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs.

Network use

Featherroll only uses the network when Photos needs to download an original from iCloud Photos to analyze it. That download goes between your device and Apple. You can keep this to Wi-Fi in the app.

Tips

Tips are optional purchases handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers those purchases.

Children

Featherroll does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. They do not contain your photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
