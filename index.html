Rollwise

Keep the frame worth keeping. Free, on-device photo cleanup for iPhone and iPad.

How do I review?

Open a category. Swipe right to keep, left to add to the queue, or up to decide later. Pinch to zoom. The tick marks the frame Rollwise would keep; you can keep a different one.

Is anything deleted automatically?

No. Queued items are only moved when you open the queue and confirm. They go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for about 30 days.

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. The scan runs on the device. See the privacy policy.

Why are some photos skipped?

Photos stored only in iCloud need to download first. Connect to Wi-Fi, or turn on iCloud on cellular in the app.

I only allowed some photos

Rollwise works with the photos you share. Tap Choose photos on the home screen to add more, or Allow all photos to review the whole library.

Tips

Rollwise is free. Tips are optional and unlock nothing. They help fund improvements.
